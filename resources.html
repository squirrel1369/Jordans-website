Guides, Care & Community

Useful things for complicated moments.

Death, grief, caregiving, and serious illness can leave you with a lot of questions and not always a clear idea of where to look for answers. This is a growing collection of practical resources, guides, organizations, books, tools, and other things I've found helpful through my work in hospice and spiritual care.

You'll find things for planning ahead, navigating grief, supporting someone who is dying, finding community, and simply making sense of some of the stranger parts of being human.

Giving Back

Community Charity Spotlight

Because conversations about care should lead to caring, a portion of future project proceeds will support programs involved in preventing people experiencing homelessness from dying on the streets by providing medical respite and end-of-life care.

These organizations provide a home environment where unhoused adults can receive hospice care from licensed hospice agencies, medical treatment, and supportive services with dignity and warmth.
