About Jordan

A hospice chaplain & writer paying attention to life.

I’m Jordan, a hospice chaplain and writer who spends a lot of time around death, and, perhaps surprisingly, a lot of time thinking about life.

Hospice has taught me what can happen when we know death is coming. There can be time for conversations, decisions, stories, apologies, laughter, and simply being together. But not every death gives us that time. Sometimes it comes suddenly, leaving families to make decisions they never expected to face and questions they never had the chance to ask.

That experience is why I care so much about talking about death before we have to.

This project is my way of making those conversations a little more approachable. I create shirts and other things about mortality, advance care planning, spirituality, grief, meaning, and the strange humor of being human. Some are thoughtful. Some are irreverent. All of them are meant to make you pause, ask a question, or start a conversation.

And because conversations about care should lead to caring, a portion of the proceeds will support organizations serving people experiencing homelessness, disability, serious illness, and the end of life.

I don't think talking about death makes life darker.
I think it helps us pay better attention to the life we have.
