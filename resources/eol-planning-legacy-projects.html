EOL Planning

Legacy Projects

— Because your family shouldn't have to guess —

End-of-life planning is about making your wishes known before someone else has to make decisions for you.

It can include the practical things like advance directives, medical decision-makers, funeral or disposition preferences, important documents, and financial or legal considerations. But it can also include the less tangible questions: What makes life worth living to you? What would you want if you couldn't speak for yourself? What does comfort mean to you? Who do you want around you?

This isn't about expecting the worst or planning for death tomorrow. It's about giving yourself—and the people you love—more clarity whenever the unexpected happens.

I'll offer resources, guides, conversation starters, and practical tools to help you figure out what matters to you and put it into words.

You don't have to know exactly what you want. You just have to start the conversation.
