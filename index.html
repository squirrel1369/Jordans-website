Welcome. I’m Jordan, a hospice chaplain and writer who spends a lot of time around death—and, perhaps surprisingly, a lot of time thinking about life.

Why This Space Exists

Making difficult conversations a little more approachable

Hospice has taught me what can happen when we know death is coming. There can be time for conversations, decisions, stories, apologies, laughter, and simply being together. But not every death gives us that time. Sometimes it comes suddenly, leaving families to make decisions they never expected to face and questions they never had the chance to ask.

That experience is why I care so much about talking about death before we have to. Here you’ll find Friday morning reflections from the bedside, practical end-of-life planning guides, and community events designed to help you pause, get curious, and start the conversation.
